Everything you need, nothing you don't

A small, focused app. It reads the receipt, asks when it is not sure, and keeps the result on your phone.

  • Scan in seconds

    Use the document camera. Merchant, date, total and currency are read on the device with Apple's text recognition.

  • Review before saving

    If a field is unclear the app leaves it empty and tells you, instead of guessing. Correct anything, then save.

  • VAT and tax ID

    Picks up the VAT amount, the tax breakdown and the merchant's tax ID, ready for your accountant.

  • Export to CSV or PDF

    Share a spreadsheet-ready CSV or a printable PDF with totals per currency. Limit it to a date range.

  • Private by design

    No account, no analytics, no tracking. The photo is discarded when you save or cancel.

  • Four languages

    English, Spanish, French and German, including receipts printed in those languages.

A look inside

Light and dark appearance, large text sizes supported.

Privacy at a glance

Receipt Scanner was built so that there is nothing to collect.

  • Text recognition runs on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework.
  • The photo of the receipt lives in memory only while you review it.
  • Receipts are stored only on your device; delete one and its data is gone.
  • If you export, you choose where the file goes through the iOS share sheet.

Questions

Is it on the App Store?

Not yet. This page will link to it at launch.

Where are my receipts stored?

Only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no sync.

The scan misread a field.

Every field can be corrected on the review screen before saving. Ambiguous fields are left empty instead of guessed.

How do I export my receipts?

Open the history, tap Export, choose CSV or PDF and, optionally, a date range. The iOS share sheet lets you save the file or send it.

Does it need internet?

No. Scanning, reading, saving and exporting all work in airplane mode.

The camera does not open.

Check Settings → Receipt Scanner → Camera. Scanning needs an iPhone with a camera.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a receipt from its detail screen, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
