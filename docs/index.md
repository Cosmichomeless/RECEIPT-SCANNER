# Receipt Scanner — Support

Receipt Scanner turns paper receipts into editable records, entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

- [Privacy policy / Política de privacidad](privacy/)
- Questions, bug reports and feature requests: <https://github.com/Cosmichomeless/RECEIPT-SCANNER/issues>

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are my receipts stored?** Only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. There is no account and no sync.

**The scan misread a field.** Every field can be corrected on the review screen before saving. Ambiguous fields are left empty instead of guessed.

**How do I export my receipts?** Open the history, tap the export button, choose CSV or PDF and, optionally, a date range. The iOS share sheet lets you save the file or send it.

**The camera does not open.** Check Settings → Receipt Scanner → Camera. The scanner needs a physical iPhone with a camera.

**How do I delete my data?** Delete a receipt from its detail screen, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
