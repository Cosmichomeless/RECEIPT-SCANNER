## Description
Receipt Scanner turns paper receipts into clean, editable records, and it does it entirely on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account to create and no ads.

SCAN
Point the camera at a receipt. The app reads it on the device with Apple's text recognition and finds the merchant, date, total, currency, VAT and the merchant's tax ID.

REVIEW BEFORE SAVING
Receipts are messy. If a field is unclear, the app leaves it empty and tells you instead of guessing. Correct anything on the review screen, then save.

YOUR HISTORY
Saved receipts stay in a simple list on your phone. Open one to see the details, or delete it whenever you like.

EXPORT
Share your receipts as a CSV (opens in Numbers and Excel) or a printable PDF with totals per currency. Limit the export to a date range for your accountant or your expense report.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Works offline, even in airplane mode
- No account, no analytics, no tracking
- The photo of the receipt is discarded when you save or cancel
- Delete a receipt and its data is gone

LANGUAGES
English, Spanish, French and German, including receipts printed in those languages.

Receipt Scanner needs a device with a camera to scan.
